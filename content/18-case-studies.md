### Section 2: Case Study Cards

*[NOTE: The current site shows placeholder case study titles ("This is the title of the case study 5/6/7/8"). These need to be replaced with real case studies. Below are recommended case study structures for content the client should provide.]*

**Recommended Case Study Template:**

Each case study should follow this structure:
- **Client type** (e.g., "Dutch retail brand" or "European toy distributor")
- **Challenge:** What problem did the client face?
- **Approach:** How did M-Asia solve it? Which services were used?
- **Result:** What was the measurable outcome? (cost savings, delivery on time, quality improvement)
- **Quote:** A client testimonial or key takeaway

**Suggested Case Study Topics (based on M-Asia's service areas):**

1. **First-time sourcing:** How a Dutch retailer sourced their first product from China with M-Asia's support — from concept to shelf in 14 weeks.
2. **Quality turnaround:** How M-Asia implemented QC inspections for a brand experiencing inconsistent quality from an existing Chinese supplier.
3. **Cost optimisation:** How purchasing guidance helped a wholesaler reduce unit costs by benchmarking against alternative factories.
4. **Toy compliance:** How M-Asia managed EN71 certification and production for a new children's product line.
5. **Logistics coordination:** How door-to-door transport management reduced shipping costs and eliminated documentation delays for a repeat client.
6. **Product development:** How a leisure brand went from rough concept to production-ready sample in three iterations.

*[ASSUMPTION: Real case study content must be provided by the client. The titles and structures above are recommendations.]*
