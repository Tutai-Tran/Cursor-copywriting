### Section 1: Agreements That Protect You Where It Matters

**Subtitle:** Contracts

**Content:**

A contract that works in the Netherlands may be unenforceable in China. Different legal systems, different languages, and different business customs mean that standard European purchase agreements often fail to protect you where it counts — at the factory.

M-Asia structures supplier agreements specifically for Chinese commercial law. Our contracts are bilingual (English and Chinese), reference Chinese jurisdiction, and include enforceable clauses for IP protection, pricing locks, quality standards, and delivery penalties.

### Section 2: What Our Contracts Cover

**Intellectual property protection:** NDA clauses, design ownership, mould ownership, and non-compete terms that prevent your supplier from selling your product to third parties.

**Pricing and payment terms:** Fixed unit pricing, payment milestones (typically 30% deposit / 70% before shipment), and conditions under which prices may be renegotiated.

**Quality standards and penalties:** Specification references, AQL acceptance levels, and penalty clauses for defective goods or delivery delays.

**Production exclusivity:** Where relevant, clauses that restrict the factory from producing identical or substantially similar products for competitors.

### Section 3: Common Contract Questions (Accordion)

**Accordion item 1: Are contracts actually enforceable in China?**
Yes — if they are structured correctly. A contract written in English only, referencing Dutch jurisdiction, has little practical enforceability in China. Our contracts are bilingual, reference Chinese jurisdiction, and follow the contract structures that Chinese courts recognise. This is the critical difference.

**Accordion item 2: Do I need a separate NDA before sharing designs?**
We recommend it. Before any detailed product information is shared with a factory, we issue an NDA that the factory signs. This is a standard step in our process and protects your design and specifications from being shared or copied.

**Accordion item 3: Can I use my own contract template?**
You can. We review client-provided templates and advise on clauses that may not be enforceable in China. In most cases, we recommend either adapting your template or using our structure as the primary agreement with your terms incorporated.

### Section 4: Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: How much does contract preparation cost?**
A: Contract costs depend on complexity. A standard supplier manufacturing agreement is included in our sourcing service fee. Specialised contracts (licensing, joint development) are quoted separately.

**Q2: Do you provide legal representation in China?**
A: We are not a law firm, but we work with Chinese legal partners when enforcement or dispute resolution is needed. Our contracts are structured to minimise the need for legal action by setting clear expectations upfront.

**Q3: What happens if a supplier breaches the contract?**
A: We first attempt resolution through direct negotiation — most disputes are resolved this way. If formal action is needed, the contract provides a clear legal pathway under Chinese jurisdiction.

**Q4: Can you help with import compliance contracts?**
A: We can incorporate compliance requirements (CE, REACH, EN71, etc.) into the supplier agreement so that the factory is contractually responsible for meeting EU product safety standards.
