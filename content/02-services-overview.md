### Section 3: Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Can I use one service without the others?**
A: Yes. Every service is available independently. Some clients only need quality control for an existing supplier, while others use our full end-to-end package. We tailor the scope to your situation.

**Q2: How do your services compare to working with a trading company?**
A: A trading company adds a margin to the factory price and typically controls the supplier relationship. We give you direct access to the factory, transparent pricing, and full ownership of the supplier contact. You build a long-term supply chain, not a dependency.

**Q3: Do you work with specific product categories only?**
A: No. Our process is product-agnostic. We work across leisure, retail, toys, and other consumer product categories. The structured approach — sourcing, sampling, QC, logistics — applies regardless of what you manufacture.

**Q4: What is the minimum order size to work with M-Asia?**
A: There is no fixed minimum on our side. Factory MOQs vary by product. During the sourcing intake we identify manufacturers whose minimums align with your volume. We are transparent about what is realistic.

**Q5: How quickly can you start?**
A: We can begin within days of receiving your brief. A sourcing scan typically takes one to two weeks. From there, timelines depend on product complexity, but we provide a clear schedule at the start of every project.
