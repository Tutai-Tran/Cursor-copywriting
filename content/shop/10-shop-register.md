### Section 4: Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Is registration really free?**
A: Yes. There is no cost to register, no annual fee, and no purchase obligation. You only pay when you place an order.

**Q2: How long does account approval take?**
A: Most accounts are approved within one business day during Dutch business hours (Monday–Friday, 9:00–17:00 CET).

**Q3: Do I need to be a registered business?**
A: Our shop is designed for B2B buyers — retailers, wholesalers, semi-wholesalers, and online sellers. We verify business registrations to maintain wholesale pricing integrity.

**Q4: Can I register from outside the Netherlands?**
A: Yes. We serve B2B buyers across Europe. Shipping costs for destinations outside the Netherlands are calculated during checkout.

**Q5: What information do I need to register?**
A: You will need your company name, contact details, business registration number (KVK or equivalent), and delivery address. The form takes less than two minutes to complete.
