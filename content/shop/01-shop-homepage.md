## FAQ Section

**Q1: Do I need to register to see prices?**
A: Yes. We offer wholesale B2B pricing that is only visible to registered buyers. Registration is free and takes less than two minutes. Once approved, you can log in and see all prices immediately.

**Q2: What is the minimum order quantity?**
A: Minimum order quantities vary by product and are displayed on each product page once you are logged in. Typical MOQs range from 60 to 200 units depending on the product.

**Q3: Where do you ship from?**
A: All products are shipped from our warehouse in the Netherlands. We offer delivery across Europe. Standard delivery in the Netherlands takes 2–3 business days.

**Q4: Are these products quality-inspected?**
A: Yes. Every product in our catalog has been quality-inspected by our team in China before shipment to the Netherlands. We check materials, construction, packaging, and compliance with EU safety standards.

**Q5: Can I also order custom or branded products?**
A: This catalog contains our ready-stock range. If you need custom products, private-label branding, or sourcing for specific items, visit our main website at masia.jkc-dev.nl or request a sourcing scan.

**Q6: How do I pay?**
A: We accept bank transfer and standard B2B payment methods. Payment details and terms are provided during checkout and on your invoice.
