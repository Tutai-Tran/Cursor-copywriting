### Section 4: Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: I cannot log in to my account. What should I do?**
A: If you have forgotten your password, use the "Lost your password?" link on the login page. If your account has not been approved yet, please allow one business day for verification. Contact us if the issue persists.

**Q2: How do I track my order?**
A: Once your order is shipped, you will receive a confirmation email with tracking information. You can also check your order status in the "My Account" section of the shop.

**Q3: Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?**
A: Contact us as soon as possible. If the order has not been shipped yet, we can usually modify or cancel it. Once shipped, changes are no longer possible.

**Q4: I received damaged products. What should I do?**
A: Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage. We will arrange a replacement or credit depending on the situation. Our products are insured during transit.

**Q5: How do I reorder previous products?**
A: Log in to your account and go to "My Account > Orders". You can view your order history and reorder items directly from there.

**Q6: Can I visit your showroom?**
A: Yes. You are welcome to visit our office in Rijen, the Netherlands, to view products in person. Please contact us in advance to schedule a visit.
