## FAQ Section

**Q1: Why can I not see prices?**
A: Wholesale prices are only visible to registered B2B buyers. Registration is free and approval is typically within one business day. Register here to get started.

**Q2: Can I order products that are not in this catalog?**
A: Yes. If you need a specific product that is not in our current catalog, contact our team. We can source it from our network of vetted Chinese manufacturers. Visit our sourcing service page for details.

**Q3: How often is the catalog updated?**
A: We add new products regularly as new shipments arrive from China. Check back frequently or contact us to be notified when new products in your category arrive.

**Q4: What are the payment terms for wholesale orders?**
A: Standard payment is via bank transfer. Payment terms are confirmed during the registration process. Invoices are provided with each order.
