### Section 2: Challenges in Toy Sourcing (Accordion)

**Subtitle:** Safety and compliance challenges we solve

**Intro text:** Toy regulations are among the strictest in consumer products. Getting them wrong means unsellable stock, recalls, or legal liability. We prevent this by selecting experienced factories, testing early, and documenting everything.

**Accordion item 1: How do you ensure EN71 compliance during production?**
We start by selecting factories with a track record of producing EN71-compliant toys. Material certificates are verified before production begins. During development, we review the product design for compliance risks (small parts, sharp edges, chemical content). Pre-shipment samples are sent to accredited EU laboratories for formal EN71 testing. Products that fail are corrected before the batch ships.

**Accordion item 2: What is GPSR and how does it affect my toy imports?**
The General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR) came into effect in December 2024 and strengthens requirements for product traceability, labelling, and documentation for all consumer products sold in the EU — including toys. M-Asia ensures that your product packaging, labels, and import documentation meet GPSR requirements, including the mandatory EU responsible person details.

**Accordion item 3: How do you handle age grading and warning labels?**
We review age grading based on the toy's intended use, features, and small-parts risk assessment. Warning labels are designed according to the Toy Safety Directive and included on packaging and product. We verify correct placement and content during pre-shipment inspection.

### Section 4: Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: What toy certifications are required for the EU market?**
A: At minimum, toys sold in the EU must comply with EN71 (parts 1, 2, and 3), REACH (restricted chemicals), and carry CE marking. Additional requirements may apply depending on the toy type — electronic toys, cosmetic-related toys, and toys for children under 36 months have extra standards.

**Q2: Do you arrange third-party lab testing?**
A: Yes. We coordinate testing at accredited EU-recognised laboratories (SGS, Bureau Veritas, TÜV, Intertek). Test samples are collected during production and sent directly to the lab. You receive the test reports and certificates before shipment.

**Q3: Can you source licensed or branded toys?**
A: We can source production of toys for which you hold the license. We do not assist with products that infringe on existing intellectual property. Licensing documentation is verified as part of our onboarding process.

**Q4: What age ranges do you cover?**
A: We source toys for all age ranges — from infant products (0–36 months, which carry the strictest regulations) to children's toys, educational products, and hobby items. Age-appropriate safety testing is applied to each.

**Q5: How do I stay compliant with changing EU regulations?**
A: We monitor regulatory updates (such as the GPSR changes) and communicate relevant changes to our clients. When new requirements take effect, we update factory briefs and inspection criteria proactively.
