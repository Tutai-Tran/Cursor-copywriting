### Section 2: Types of Inspections We Perform

**Pre-production inspection:** We verify that raw materials, components, and production setup match your approved specifications before manufacturing begins. This prevents problems from being built into the entire batch.

**During-production inspection (DUPRO):** At 20–40% production completion, we check the first finished units against your sample and spec sheet. This is the most cost-effective point to catch and correct issues.

**Pre-shipment inspection (PSI):** When production is complete and goods are packed, we perform a final inspection based on AQL sampling. We check product quality, packaging, labelling, and quantity before authorising shipment.

**Factory audit:** Before starting production with a new supplier, we audit their facility, management systems, equipment, and workforce. This confirms their capability matches your requirements.

### Section 5: Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: What is AQL and how does it apply to my products?**
A: AQL (Acceptable Quality Level) is a statistical sampling method used to determine how many units to inspect and what defect levels are acceptable. We use internationally recognised AQL tables (typically AQL 2.5 for major defects, AQL 4.0 for minor) and adjust based on your tolerance.

**Q2: How quickly can you arrange an inspection?**
A: We can schedule an inspection within two to five business days, depending on factory location and production status. For ongoing production runs, we set up a recurring inspection schedule.

**Q3: Do I receive photos and reports?**
A: Yes. Every inspection includes a detailed report with photos, measurements, defect classifications, and a pass/hold/fail recommendation. Reports are sent within 24 hours of the inspection.

**Q4: Can you test products for EU compliance?**
A: We coordinate third-party lab testing for CE marking, EN71 (toys), REACH, and other EU compliance requirements. Test samples are collected during production and sent to accredited laboratories. Certificates are provided before shipment.

**Q5: What does quality control cost?**
A: QC costs depend on inspection type, factory location, and order size. We provide a clear quote per inspection before you commit. Most clients find that the cost of inspections is a fraction of what a quality failure would cost at the warehouse or retail level.
