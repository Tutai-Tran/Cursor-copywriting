### Section 3: Content Strategy Recommendations

**Priority 1 — Compliance and regulation articles (informational, high SEO value):**
1. "What is GPSR and what does it mean for importers from China?" — Targets the new General Product Safety Regulation. Coolenchina.com already covers this topic; M-Asia should too.
2. "EN71 explained: toy safety requirements for EU importers" — Evergreen compliance guide.
3. "REACH compliance for products from China: what you need to know" — Chemical safety requirements.
4. "CE marking for imported products: a practical guide" — Common questions answered.

**Priority 2 — Practical sourcing guides (informational, funnel to services):**
5. "How to find a reliable manufacturer in China: step-by-step" — High-volume keyword, positions M-Asia process as the solution.
6. "What is a sourcing agent and do you need one?" — Defines the service category M-Asia operates in.
7. "How much does it cost to source products from China?" — Targets cost-related queries, builds transparency positioning.
8. "5 mistakes European brands make when sourcing from China" — Engaging format, links to relevant services.

**Priority 3 — Seasonal and timely content (timely, social sharing value):**
9. "How to prepare your supply chain for Chinese New Year [year]" — Annual content, refreshed yearly. Coolenchina.com covers this topic.
10. "Shipping delays from China: causes and how to plan around them" — Expands on the current weather-delay notification.

**Priority 4 — Sector-specific articles (informational, topical depth):**
11. "Sourcing toys from China: compliance checklist for EU importers" — Combines sector + compliance.
12. "Retail sourcing from China: managing seasonal production timelines" — Targets retail buyers specifically.

**Publishing cadence recommendation:** 2 articles per month to build consistent topical authority. Each article should include:
- Internal links to at least 2 service pages
- A CTA to the sourcing scan request
- Schema markup (Article schema)
- An estimated reading time
