### Section 2: Why Sourcing Fails Without a System (Challenges Accordion)

**Subtitle:** Common sourcing problems we solve

**Intro text:** Many teams struggle with shifting prices, unclear specs, and slow replies across time zones. The result is missed launch windows and inconsistent quality. We solve this with clear documentation, a fixed update cadence, and factory visits when decisions matter.

**Accordion item 1: How do you prevent price changes after quoting?**
We lock pricing in writing with the factory before production starts. Our quotes include material costs, labour, tooling, and packaging — itemised so you can see where every euro goes. If raw material prices shift, we notify you with evidence and options before any cost changes take effect.

**Accordion item 2: What if the factory cannot deliver on time?**
We validate production capacity during factory audits before placing an order. Lead times are agreed in the contract, and our China team monitors production milestones weekly. If delays arise, we flag them early with a revised timeline and alternative actions so your launch plan stays intact.

**Accordion item 3: How do you vet factories to ensure they are legitimate?**
We visit every factory in person. We check business licences, production equipment, workforce size, existing client references, and quality management systems. We do not work with trading companies posing as manufacturers. You receive a factory audit report summarising our findings before you approve any supplier.

### Section 4: Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: How many suppliers do you typically shortlist?**
A: We typically present three to five vetted factories per product. Each comes with a capability summary, price comparison, lead time estimate, and our recommendation. You make the final choice.

**Q2: Can you source products I have already designed?**
A: Yes. If you have finished designs, CAD files, or technical drawings, we start directly at the supplier validation stage. We match your specifications to factories with the right equipment and materials.

**Q3: Do I own the supplier relationship?**
A: Absolutely. We introduce you to the factory, and you maintain direct ownership of that relationship. We never lock you into dependency on M-Asia for reorders.

**Q4: What products can you source?**
A: We source across consumer product categories including retail goods, toys, leisure equipment, home products, and promotional items. If it is manufactured in China, we can find the right factory.

**Q5: What does a sourcing scan include?**
A: A sourcing scan evaluates your product idea, target pricing, volume, and timeline. We identify suitable manufacturer types, flag risks (certifications, tooling, MOQs), and outline a realistic project plan. It is the foundation for every successful sourcing project.

**Q6: How long does it take to find a supplier?**
A: From brief to shortlist typically takes two to four weeks, depending on product complexity. Simple products with standard materials are faster. Custom-engineered products with specific certifications require more due diligence.
