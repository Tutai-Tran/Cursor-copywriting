### Section 2: Transport Options Compared

**Sea freight (FCL/LCL):** The most cost-effective option for large volumes. Transit time from China to the Netherlands is typically 30–40 days. We arrange full container loads (FCL) or less-than-container-load (LCL) consolidation depending on your volume.

**Rail freight:** A middle ground between sea and air. Transit time is approximately 18–22 days from China to Europe. Suitable for medium-urgency shipments where air freight costs are not justified.

**Air freight:** The fastest option at 5–10 days transit time. Best for high-value, low-volume, or time-critical shipments. We compare carriers and consolidators to find the best rate for your shipment size.

### Section 3: Common Logistics Challenges (Accordion)

**Subtitle:** Problems we solve in transport

**Intro text:** Logistics delays, unexpected costs, and documentation errors are among the most common frustrations in China imports. We prevent them with upfront quoting, experienced forwarders, and end-to-end oversight.

**Accordion item 1: How do you prevent shipping delays?**
We coordinate production timelines with shipping schedules to avoid idle time between factory completion and container departure. Our China team supervises loading and confirms goods are on board before the vessel sails. If delays occur at port, we notify you immediately with alternatives.

**Accordion item 2: What export documentation do you handle?**
We prepare or verify commercial invoices, packing lists, bills of lading, certificates of origin, and any product-specific certificates (CE, test reports). All documents are checked for accuracy before submission to customs, reducing clearance delays.

**Accordion item 3: Can you arrange delivery directly to my customer?**
Yes. We can ship directly to your end customer or distribution centre anywhere in Europe. Drop-shipping and split deliveries are supported. We coordinate last-mile logistics as part of the transport plan.

### Section 4: Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: How long does sea freight from China to the Netherlands take?**
A: Typical transit time is 30–40 days port to port, depending on the departure port in China and the route. Add 3–5 days for inland transport on each end. We provide the exact schedule with your transport quote.

**Q2: Do you handle customs clearance in Europe?**
A: We coordinate with customs brokers in the Netherlands (or your destination country) to ensure smooth clearance. We prepare the required documentation in advance to minimise delays and avoid duties surprises.

**Q3: Can I combine transport with your other services?**
A: Yes. Most clients combine transport with quality control and sourcing. This way, we supervise loading after the pre-shipment inspection and handle logistics as a single workflow.

**Q4: How are transport costs calculated?**
A: Costs depend on shipment volume (CBM or weight), transport mode (sea, rail, air), origin and destination, and current market rates. We provide an itemised quote — freight cost, documentation fees, insurance, and local delivery — before you commit.

**Q5: Do you offer cargo insurance?**
A: Yes. We can arrange all-risk cargo insurance through our logistics partners. We recommend insurance for all shipments and provide a quote as part of the transport proposal.
