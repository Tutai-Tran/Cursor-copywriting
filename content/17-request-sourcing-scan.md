## Main Content Sections

### Section 1: What Is a Sourcing Scan?

**Content:**

A sourcing scan is your starting point. You describe your product, target market, volume, and timeline. We evaluate the feasibility, identify suitable manufacturer types in China, flag potential risks (certifications, tooling, MOQs), and outline a realistic project plan.

The scan gives you clarity before you commit resources. You will know what is possible, what it will roughly cost, and how long it will take — based on our experience with similar products.

There is no cost and no obligation. We treat every submission as confidential.

### Section 3: What Happens Next

**Content:**

After you submit the form:

1. **Review (1–2 business days):** We review your product details and research suitable manufacturer types.
2. **Initial assessment:** We send you a brief report outlining feasibility, estimated pricing range, lead time, and any risks or compliance requirements.
3. **Follow-up call:** If the scan looks promising, we schedule a call to discuss your project in detail and agree on next steps.

There is no commitment at any stage. You decide whether to proceed based on the information we provide.
