## Main Content Sections

### Section 1: Your Independent Link to Asia

**Subtitle:** About

**Tagline:** "We connect European brands directly to vetted manufacturers"

**Content:**

M-Asia connects European brands to vetted manufacturers and manages the full path from brief to first shipment. We filter traders, verify factory capabilities, and translate your requirements into factory actions.

Our clients work with one point of contact in the Netherlands while specialists in China handle sourcing, sampling, quality control, and logistics. You get clarity, speed, and documentation that supports repeat orders.

We are independent. We do not own factories, we do not take commissions on orders, and we do not have exclusive supplier arrangements that bias our recommendations. Every factory we suggest has been evaluated on merit — capability, pricing, quality track record, and capacity.

The result is a supply chain you control, built on relationships you own, with a partner who earns trust through performance.

### Section 2: Our History — From First Brief to Worldwide Scale

**Timeline content:**

**2005 — The Starting Point**
Vinod Gopani started M-Asia with a clear goal: give European brands speed and control in Asian manufacturing. The idea was simple — one clear process, two committed teams, and full transparency for the client.

**2008 — First Cross-Continent Projects**
Early clients trusted M-Asia with seasonal product launches. We built a disciplined routine around specifications, request-for-quotations, and comparable quotes that set the standard for our work.

**2012 — China Team on the Ground**
We expanded our China operations with a permanent office in Guangzhou. Being at factories when it matters — during sampling, audits, and inspections — made our quality more consistent and our response times faster.

**2016 — QC Playbook Formalised**
We translated client specifications into measurable inspection checklists with AQL standards and clear pass-or-hold rules. Quality stopped being a guess and became a documented, repeatable process.

**2019 — Sectors at Scale**
We refined our approach for leisure, retail, and toys without locking into narrow product niches. The same structured process, tuned for the specific compliance, seasonality, and margin requirements of each sector.

**2026 — The Next Chapter**
We continue to refine speed, predictability, and documentation while staying independent. The goal is calm product launches and long partnerships built on trust and results.

### Section 3: Why Choose M-Asia?

**Subtitle:** Why Us

**Intro text:** Skip the detours. With M-Asia you brief once, we open the right factory door, and the goods move without extra hands getting in the way. Decisions are faster, prices stay clear, and quality is checked where it matters.

**Reason 1: Independent and Transparent**
We do not own factories or take supplier commissions. Our income comes from clear service fees, so our recommendations are always in your interest. You see the factory price, our fee, and logistics costs separately.

**Reason 2: Teams in Both Countries**
Our Dutch team understands your market, regulations, and business culture. Our Chinese team understands factory operations, negotiations, and local logistics. Together they bridge the gap that causes most sourcing problems.

**Reason 3: One Point of Contact**
You work with one dedicated person who knows your product, your timeline, and your quality standards. Behind that contact is a team, but your experience is personal and consistent.

**Reason 4: Your Supply Chain, Your Control**
We introduce you to factories, not the other way around. You own the supplier relationship. If you outgrow our services or want to manage in-house, you can — without losing your supply chain.

**Reason 5: Structured Process, Documented Results**
Every step is documented — from sourcing brief to inspection report to shipping confirmation. This gives you an auditable trail and makes reorders predictable.

**Reason 6: 21+ Years of Experience**
We have handled over 200 containers per year across multiple product categories. Our experience means we anticipate problems before they occur and resolve issues faster when they do.

### Section 5: Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Is M-Asia a trading company?**
A: No. M-Asia is an independent sourcing and production management company. We do not buy and resell products (except through our trading service). Our primary role is to connect you directly with vetted manufacturers and manage the process on your behalf.

**Q2: Where are your offices?**
A: Our Dutch office is in Rijen, the Netherlands (Ericssonstraat 2, 5121 ML). Our China office is in Guangzhou (Jinzhou International Business Hotel, No. 899 Jiefang North Road, 510040).

**Q3: How many people work at M-Asia?**
A: We have teams in both countries, with dedicated staff for sourcing, quality control, logistics, and client management. Our size allows us to be hands-on and responsive — you are never just a ticket number.

**Q4: Do you work with startups or only established brands?**
A: We work with companies of all sizes, from startups launching their first product to established brands optimising their supply chain. The process is the same; the scope is tailored to your needs.

**Q5: Can I visit your offices or join a factory trip?**
A: Yes. You are welcome to visit our Netherlands office or join a guided factory trip in China. We can arrange supplier visits, production reviews, and market exploration tours.
