### Section 2: Challenges in Retail Sourcing (Accordion)

**Subtitle:** Common challenges we solve for retail brands

**Intro text:** Retail margins leave little room for error. Factory price increases, late deliveries, and inconsistent quality directly impact your bottom line. We prevent these issues with proactive planning, transparent pricing, and on-the-ground quality control.

**Accordion item 1: How do you ensure consistent product quality across repeat orders?**
We maintain a detailed specification file for every product, including materials, dimensions, colours (Pantone), packaging layout, and labelling requirements. This file is referenced for every reorder, and our QC team inspects against the approved standard. Variation is tracked and addressed immediately.

**Accordion item 2: Can you source private-label and branded products?**
Yes. We coordinate private-label production including custom packaging, branding, barcode labelling, and retailer-specific requirements (e.g., Bol.com, Amazon, or brick-and-mortar chain specs). We ensure all branding elements are correct before production begins.

**Accordion item 3: How do you handle tight retail deadlines?**
We work backwards from your sell-in date to create a production calendar that includes factory booking, sample approval, production, QC, and shipping. Chinese holiday periods and peak production seasons are factored in. If the timeline is tight, we identify express production or air freight options.

### Section 3: Our Process for Retail Products

**Step 1 — Sourcing Intake:** We review your product range, retail requirements, margin targets, and sell-in dates to create a sourcing brief.

**Step 2 — Supplier Validation:** Factories are compared on unit price, production speed, packaging capability, and experience with retail-grade products.

**Step 3 — Sampling and Engineering:** We produce samples including final packaging, branding, and labelling. Samples are approved before bulk production starts.

### Section 4: Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: What retail product categories do you source?**
A: We source consumer goods, household products, seasonal items, gift and promotional products, accessories, and private-label ranges. The common thread is products manufactured in China for sale through retail channels in Europe.

**Q2: Can you meet retailer compliance requirements (e.g., for Bol.com or Amazon)?**
A: Yes. We are familiar with the product listing, labelling, and packaging requirements of major online and offline retailers. These requirements are included in the factory brief and verified during inspection.

**Q3: How do you manage currency and price fluctuations?**
A: We quote in the agreed currency and lock factory pricing before production. If significant raw material or exchange rate shifts occur, we notify you with evidence and options — never surprise price increases.

**Q4: What MOQs should I expect for retail products?**
A: MOQs vary by product and factory. Simple products may start at 500–1,000 units. Custom-tooled or branded items may require higher volumes. We negotiate MOQs based on your volume and present alternatives when minimums are above your buy depth.
