### Section 2: What Purchasing Guidance Covers

**Cost analysis and benchmarking:** We review your current supplier pricing against market rates for comparable products. This reveals whether you are overpaying and identifies leverage points for negotiation.

**Payment terms and risk management:** We advise on payment structures (deposits, LC, escrow), trade credit, and how to reduce financial exposure when working with Chinese suppliers.

**MOQ optimisation:** We help you negotiate minimum order quantities that align with your sales volume and cash flow. Where MOQs are firm, we suggest alternative factories or product configurations that bring them within reach.

**Production calendar planning:** We map your product launch dates against Chinese production schedules, including factory holidays (Chinese New Year, Golden Week) and seasonal demand peaks. This prevents late deliveries and rush charges.

**Supplier relationship strategy:** We advise on how to structure long-term supplier relationships — including pricing review cycles, volume commitments, and performance benchmarks — so that your supply chain improves over time.

### Section 3: Common Purchasing Questions (Accordion)

**Accordion item 1: How do I know if my supplier's pricing is fair?**
We benchmark your prices against comparable quotes from other verified factories. This gives you a clear picture of where you stand and specific data to negotiate improvements. Many clients discover 10–20% savings potential on their first review.

**Accordion item 2: When should I start planning Chinese New Year production?**
Ideally four to five months before Chinese New Year (which falls in January or February). Factories ramp down in the weeks before the holiday and take two to four weeks to fully resume. Orders placed too late either miss the window or incur rush charges.

**Accordion item 3: Can you help me reduce my minimum order quantity?**
We negotiate MOQs based on long-term volume potential, multi-SKU orders, or timing flexibility. In some cases, we recommend alternative factories with lower MOQs or suggest product adjustments (standard colours, simpler packaging) that make smaller runs viable.

### Section 5: Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Is purchasing guidance a one-time service or ongoing?**
A: Both options are available. Some clients engage us for a one-time purchasing review to optimise their current setup. Others use ongoing guidance as their supply chain evolves, with quarterly reviews and strategy sessions.

**Q2: Do I need purchasing guidance if I already use your sourcing service?**
A: Purchasing advice is built into our sourcing process. Standalone purchasing guidance is designed for clients who have existing suppliers and want to improve their commercial terms without changing their sourcing setup.

**Q3: What does purchasing guidance cost?**
A: We offer purchasing guidance at a fixed advisory fee, scoped to the number of products or suppliers reviewed. You receive a clear proposal before the work begins, with no hidden costs.

**Q4: Can you attend supplier meetings on my behalf?**
A: Yes. Our China team can represent you in supplier negotiations, factory visits, and trade fairs. We prepare negotiation strategies in advance and debrief you on outcomes and recommendations.

**Q5: How much can I realistically save with purchasing guidance?**
A: Savings vary by product and current supplier situation. Many clients discover 10–20% cost reduction through price benchmarking alone. Additional savings come from optimised payment terms, reduced rush charges through better production planning, and lower logistics costs through smarter shipping timing.

**Q6: Can you help me reduce dependency on a single supplier?**
A: Yes. We can identify and qualify alternative factories as backup suppliers. Having a second source reduces risk and gives you negotiation leverage with your primary factory. We help you onboard backup suppliers without disrupting your current production.

**Q7: What if I am a first-time importer with no existing suppliers?**
A: Purchasing guidance is valuable at any stage. For first-time importers, we focus on helping you understand pricing structures, avoid common pitfalls (unrealistic MOQs, unfavourable payment terms), and set up your procurement process correctly from the start. This can save you significant time and money on your first order.
