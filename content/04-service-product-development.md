### Section 2: Common Development Challenges (Accordion)

**Subtitle:** Problems we prevent during development

**Intro text:** Development projects stall when communication breaks down between your design intent and what the factory can actually produce. We bridge that gap with clear documentation, regular sample reviews, and engineering input from the production side.

**Accordion item 1: How do you handle design changes during development?**
Every change request is documented in a revision log with the associated cost and timeline impact. We do not implement changes without your written approval. This prevents scope creep and keeps the project budget under control.

**Accordion item 2: What if the factory cannot produce what I designed?**
We identify manufacturability issues early — before tooling is cut. Our China team reviews your design against the factory's equipment and capabilities, then suggests practical adjustments that maintain your design intent while ensuring reliable production.

**Accordion item 3: How do you ensure sample accuracy?**
We issue detailed specification sheets with dimensions, materials, colours (Pantone references), and functional requirements. Each sample is checked against these specs by our QC team in China. Discrepancies are documented with photos and corrected before the next iteration.

### Section 4: Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Can you develop a product from just an idea or sketch?**
A: Yes. We work with everything from rough sketches to detailed CAD files. If you start with a concept, we help refine it into technical drawings and specifications that a factory can work with.

**Q2: How many sample rounds does a typical project need?**
A: Most products require two to four sample rounds. Simple products may only need one or two. Complex items with custom tooling, electronics, or specific certifications may need more. We set expectations at the start.

**Q3: Who owns the tooling and moulds?**
A: You do. Tooling paid for by you belongs to you. We include this in the factory agreement. If you decide to switch suppliers in the future, the moulds can be transferred.

**Q4: What is the cost of product development?**
A: Costs depend on product complexity. Sample costs typically range from a few hundred to several thousand euros, depending on materials and tooling. We provide a clear cost estimate at the sourcing intake before any spending is committed.

**Q5: Can you help with packaging design as well?**
A: Yes. We coordinate packaging development alongside the product itself. This includes material selection, structural design, print artwork preparation, and compliance with retail or e-commerce packaging requirements.
