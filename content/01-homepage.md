### Section 6: Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: What types of products can M-Asia source from China?**
A: We source across a wide range of categories including consumer goods, retail products, leisure items, and toys. If your product can be manufactured in China, we can find and vet the right factory for it. We work with branded and non-branded items alike.

**Q2: How does M-Asia protect my intellectual property?**
A: We use legally reviewed NDA and supplier agreements drafted for Chinese commercial law. Our contracts include IP clauses, non-compete terms, and production exclusivity where needed. We never share your designs or specifications without your written approval.

**Q3: What is a sourcing scan and is it free?**
A: A sourcing scan is a focused assessment where we evaluate your product idea, target price, and timeline. We identify suitable manufacturers, flag potential risks, and outline a realistic production plan. Request one through our website to get started.

**Q4: Do I need to travel to China?**
A: No. Our China team handles factory visits, sample reviews, and production inspections on your behalf. You stay in control through regular updates, photo reports, and video calls. If you want to visit, we can arrange guided factory tours.

**Q5: What are typical lead times for sourcing a new product?**
A: Lead times depend on product complexity. A simple product can move from brief to first shipment in 8–12 weeks. Complex products with custom tooling or certifications may take 16–20 weeks. We provide a realistic timeline during the sourcing intake.

**Q6: How does pricing work? Do you charge commission on orders?**
A: We work with transparent service fees, not hidden commissions. You see the factory price, our service cost, and logistics expenses separately. This way you can plan your margins accurately and compare costs year over year.

**Q7: Can M-Asia help if I already have a supplier in China?**
A: Yes. We can audit your existing supplier, benchmark pricing against alternatives, improve communication, and add quality control inspections. Many clients start with purchasing guidance to optimise their current setup before expanding.

**Q8: Which sectors does M-Asia specialise in?**
A: We serve three core sectors — leisure, retail, and toys — but our sourcing process is product-agnostic. The same structured approach works across categories. If your product is manufactured in China, we can support you.
