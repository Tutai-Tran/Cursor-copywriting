### Section 2: How Trading Works

**Step 1 — Brief:** You describe your product requirements, target quantity, and timeline. We advise on feasibility, pricing range, and realistic delivery dates.

**Step 2 — Quote and factory selection:** We source from our vetted manufacturer network and present a complete quote including product cost, QC, and freight. You approve before we proceed.

**Step 3 — Production and QC:** We place the order with the factory, monitor production, and perform quality inspections before shipment.

**Step 4 — Delivery:** Goods are shipped to your warehouse or customer with full documentation. You receive one invoice from M-Asia covering the entire order.

### Section 3: Common Trading Questions (Accordion)

**Accordion item 1: What is the difference between trading and sourcing?**
With sourcing, you build a direct relationship with the factory and M-Asia acts as your intermediary and project manager. With trading, M-Asia handles the entire purchase — you buy from us, not directly from the factory. Trading is simpler but gives you less visibility into factory-level pricing.

**Accordion item 2: Is trading more expensive than direct sourcing?**
Trading includes a service margin because we take on the commercial relationship and purchase risk. However, our margins are transparent and competitive. For clients who value simplicity and speed over direct factory control, trading offers better value when you factor in the time and complexity saved.

**Accordion item 3: Can I switch from trading to direct sourcing later?**
Yes. Many clients start with trading to test a product or market, then transition to our sourcing service when volumes grow. We facilitate the introduction to the factory and support the transition.

### Section 4: Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: What is the minimum order value for trading?**
A: Factory MOQs still apply, but we can often combine smaller orders or suggest alternative products with lower minimums. We advise on realistic quantities during the initial brief.

**Q2: Do I have visibility into product quality?**
A: Yes. We apply the same QC standards as our sourcing service — pre-shipment inspections, photo reports, and a pass/hold recommendation. You approve before we ship.

**Q3: Can I request custom branding or packaging?**
A: Yes. We coordinate custom branding, packaging design, and labelling as part of the trading process. This is included in the project scope and quoted upfront.

**Q4: What happens if there is a quality problem after delivery?**
A: We stand behind the products we trade. If goods arrive with quality issues that were not caught during inspection, we work with the factory to resolve the matter — whether through replacement, credit, or rework on the next order.

**Q5: How long does a trading order take from request to delivery?**
A: Timelines depend on whether the product is in stock at the factory or needs to be produced. Standard products can be shipped within 4–6 weeks. Custom or seasonal products may take 8–12 weeks. We provide a clear timeline before you confirm the order.

**Q6: Do I receive EU-compliant documentation with my order?**
A: Yes. We ensure that all traded products come with the required documentation — test reports, certificates of compliance, commercial invoices, and packing lists. For products requiring CE marking or EN71 testing, we coordinate lab testing and supply the certificates.

**Q7: Can I use trading for a one-time import?**
A: Absolutely. Trading is ideal for one-time purchases. You do not need an ongoing contract or commitment. Many clients use trading for a single order to test a product or market before deciding on repeat orders.
